### Changed

- **The verify shards split the test projects by recorded weight, not by position.** The Windows lane
  recorded 561 s, 900 s, 162 s and 467 s, with shard 2/4 at 43 % of the lane because it owned
  `Lunate.Coding.Tests` and five small projects. The shard scheduler now places one unit per culture
  pass of one project, largest recorded weight first, so both passes of a heavy project land in
  different shards and shard 1 carries the recorded cost of the publish, budgets and roundtrips on top
  of its tests. The weights live in `scripts/verify-shard-weights.tsv`, recorded by every gate run
  through the new `scripts/shard-weights.sh` and folded back with `weights --from` rather than
  estimated; `scripts/gate-tests.sh` now checks completeness, non-overlap, determinism and that no
  shard is over a third of its lane at four shards (#1043).
