### Changed

- **The verify shards split the test projects by recorded weight, not by position.** The Windows lane
  recorded 561 s, 900 s, 162 s and 467 s, with shard 2/4 at 43 % of the lane because it owned
  `Lunate.Coding.Tests` and five small projects. The shard scheduler now places one unit per culture
  pass of one project, largest recorded weight first, so both passes of a heavy project land in
  different shards and no shard carries a whole slow project. On the recorded table the Ubuntu and
  Windows pole is 826 s of 3202 s (25.8 %, inside the third) and the Windows pole becomes build plus one
  390 s pass instead of two. The weights live in `scripts/verify-shard-weights.tsv`, recorded by every
  gate run through the new `scripts/shard-weights.sh` and folded back with `weights --from` rather than
  estimated; `scripts/gate-tests.sh` checks completeness, non-overlap, determinism and the third at the
  Ubuntu and Windows shard count. macOS keeps its two-shard cap: within two shards the weight partition
  is no heavier than the position one (the gate reports 396 s against 452 s on the recorded table), and
  each shard's two culture passes now record separately, so a refresh folds the slower pass instead of
  losing one (#1043).
