### Changed

- **The verify shards split the test projects by recorded weight, not by position.** The Windows lane
  recorded 561 s, 900 s, 162 s and 467 s, with shard 2/4 at 43 % of the lane because it owned
  `Lunate.Coding.Tests` and five small projects. The shard scheduler now places one unit per culture
  pass of one project, largest recorded weight first, so both passes of a heavy project land in
  different shards and no shard carries a whole slow project. On the recorded table the Ubuntu and
  Windows four-shard loads are 736 s, 735 s, 735 s and 736 s of 2942 s, a pole of 25.0 % (inside the
  third), and the wall-time pole the partition predicts drops from the position partition's 960 s to
  602 s; on the Windows lane's own numbers the 900 s pole (build plus two ~390 s `Lunate.Coding.Tests`
  passes) becomes build plus one pass. The weights live in `scripts/verify-shard-weights.tsv`, recorded
  by every gate run through the new `scripts/shard-weights.sh` and folded back with `weights --from`
  rather than estimated; `scripts/gate-tests.sh` checks completeness, non-overlap, determinism and the
  third at the Ubuntu and Windows shard count. macOS keeps its two-shard cap: within two shards the
  weight partition's busiest shard carries 1471 s against the position partition's 2350 s and predicts
  an 886 s wall pole against 960 s (the gate reports both), and each shard's two culture passes now
  record separately, so a refresh folds the slower pass instead of losing one (#1043).
