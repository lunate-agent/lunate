### Added

- **A pull request reports its own mechanical readiness findings.** The advisory `pre-ready` job runs
  the committed `scripts/pre-ready.py` and fails, with a message naming the fix, when an OpenSpec
  change is not archived in the final commit, `changes/<pr-number>.md` is missing or `CHANGELOG.md`
  was edited directly, an `openspec/specs/` change has no archive beside it, or an acceptance check
  in the linked issue is unanswered or unmet. The job is not a required check yet: a maintainer
  enables it after a green run, and the lane script retires in a separate change (#847).
