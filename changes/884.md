### Added

- **The Mermaid renderer is its own Lunate-free library.** `Mermaid.Terminal` packages a C# port of
  mermaid-ascii's flowchart and sequence layout and drawing — Unicode and ASCII glyph sets, the
  frame/label runs a host paints separately, and the source-plus-reason fallback for unsupported
  input — with its own README, MIT licence credit and release package. `Lunate.Tui` consumes it as a
  project reference and keeps only the fence adapter (#884).

### Changed

- **Mermaid diagrams are laid out by the ported renderer.** Box borders line up, arrows attach to
  their edges and the TUI and Provider APIs boxes no longer overlap; the Unicode drawing uses
  mermaid-ascii's `┌ ┐ └ ┘ ─ │` frame, `┄`/`┆` dotted strokes and `► ▼ ◄ ▲` heads, while ASCII keeps
  `+ - |` and `< > ^ v`. The documented parse, unsupported-type and width fallbacks and the
  40-column floor are unchanged (#884).
