### Changed

- **Every CI job is bounded by a timeout, so a hung step can no longer hold a runner for six hours.**
  No job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` set `timeout-minutes`, so a stuck test kept its runner — and
  the pull request behind it — for GitHub's default of 360 minutes; the `verify` shards were seen
  waiting over an hour on steps whose own wall time is 3–12 minutes. Each job now carries a number
  about three times its measured duration: the verify legs take theirs from the plan's matrix, 30
  minutes on Ubuntu (the #1045 shard's 12m19s), 45 on Windows and 120 on the bimodal macOS runner,
  with `verify-powershell` at 60, `perf` at 90, `platform` at 12 and a five-minute floor for the
  sub-minute jobs. A comment at the top of the file records every number with the runs it was
  measured from, so the next change to a job can move its timeout with it (#1049).
